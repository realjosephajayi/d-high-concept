Frequently asked questions

Quick answers before you enroll.

Do I need any experience?

No. Our programs are built for every starting point, from total beginners to professionals adding a new skill. Beginner friendly courses are tagged on the page so you know where to start.

How long does a course take?

Each course runs for 4 weeks. You learn step by step and build a project along the way.

How much does it cost?

Your fee is subsidised. We are giving out scholarships, so what you pay is a subsidised amount, not the full cost of the training. Flexible payment plans are also available. Message us to get the exact price for your course.

How do I pay?

Once you pick a course, you enroll and pay securely through Paystack or Flutterwave.

Can I learn on my phone?

Yes. The learning experience is mobile-friendly, and the CapCut course is taught on the CapCut mobile interface.

Will I get a certificate?

Yes. You finish with a certificate and a portfolio of your own work as proof of your skill.

Will I get support while I learn?

Yes. You get one-on-one guidance from industry experts, plus a community group for support.
